ALPHA · PRIVACY & DATA POLICY

Privacy & data policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Summary

Alpha is a local-first chess-analysis app. It does not require an account and does not ask for Chess.com or Lichess passwords, cookies, or access tokens. It has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Chess analysis runs on your device.

Information Alpha processes

Alpha processes chess positions and PGNs you enter or import; public Chess.com or Lichess usernames you choose to enter; the public games returned for those usernames; chess-analysis results; and presentation preferences such as board theme, piece style, and board orientation.

Imported games and analysis stay in the active app session. Presentation preferences are stored locally on the device. Alpha has no Alpha account, cloud backup, or developer-operated game database.

Network use

Board analysis and locally imported PGNs work offline. Alpha contacts a service only when you explicitly import public games: Chess.com at https://api.chess.com/pub/ or Lichess at https://lichess.org/api/. The public username you enter is part of that request.

Alpha does not proxy those requests or send imported games, engine evaluations, or chess analysis to an Alpha-operated server. Chess.com and Lichess process requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

Information Alpha does not collect

  • Passwords, platform logins, cookies, or personal access tokens;
  • Payment-card or bank information;
  • Contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera data;
  • Advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or crash-reporting telemetry; or
  • Private Chess.com or Lichess games.

Retention and deletion

Clear Alpha's Android storage or uninstall the app to remove its local application data. Closing the active session removes imported games and analysis held only in that session.

Changes and support

If Alpha's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Report privacy or security concerns through the Alpha GitHub issue tracker.
